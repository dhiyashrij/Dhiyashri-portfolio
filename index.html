About Me

Fusing Engineering Curiosity with Physical Hardware

An enthusiastic journey from foundational school science into the dynamic discipline of Electronics & Communication Engineering.

I am a first-year undergraduate student pursuing my Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Chennai Institute of Technology (CIT), having completed my schooling at Velammal Vidhyashram.

My fascination lies at the intersection where logical software instructions manifest as physical electrical voltages. I am captivated by how silicon microchips, microcontrollers, and electronic circuits can sense, compute, and physically interact with our everyday environment.

Rather than just writing abstract code on a screen, I love breadboarding circuits, debugging sensor signals with multimeters, and understanding how electronic components communicate over protocols like I2C and UART. As a first-year student, I am dedicated to building rock-solid foundations in C programming, mathematics, circuit analysis, and microcontrollers.

Long-Term Vision & Engineering Aspirations
  • Pursue advanced higher studies abroad in Embedded Systems & Semiconductor Architecture.
  • Participate in international academic research internships and hardware innovation symposiums.
  • Design meaningful, production-reliable hardware solutions for industrial automation and edge computing.

Curious Learner

Constantly exploring how low-level hardware interacts with code. I dive deep into datasheets, microcontroller architectures, and foundational C and Python programming with eager curiosity.

Hardware-Oriented Thinker

Drawn to tangible electronics: breadboard prototyping, sensor interfacing, and timing diagrams. I prioritize understanding circuit constraints and electrical integrity over high-level abstractions.

Consistent Problem Solver

Hardware debugging requires patience and rigorous method. Whether resolving motor driver noise or diagnosing loose jumpers, I isolate variables methodically to reach dependable solutions.

Hardware Prototype

Obstacle-Avoidance RC Car

An autonomous ground robotics chassis engineered with an HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor and an L298N dual H-bridge motor driver. It continuously calculates forward obstacle clearance vectors and automatically performs directional trajectory pivots when safe thresholds are breached.

Hardware & Technologies Used:
Arduino Uno HC-SR04 Ultrasonic L298N H-Bridge Embedded C/C++ PWM Speed Tuning

Intelligent Controller

Adaptive Traffic Signal with Emergency Vehicle Priority

An intelligent intersection traffic controller that dynamically scales green signal intervals based on real-time lane vehicle density, paired with an emergency vehicle preemption radio/sensor system that instantly clears a safe green corridor for ambulances and fire engines.

Sensors & Wearable IoT
Wearable Hardware Build

Industrial Worker Safety Band

An ergonomic wearable telemetry wristband built for hazardous plant operations. Continuously monitors real-time worker pulse rate and body temperature, detects toxic industrial gases (CO / Methane), and identifies sudden fall impacts via a 3-axis accelerometer to dispatch automated emergency distress alerts.

Hardware & Technologies Used:
ESP32 / Arduino Nano MPU-6050 Accelerometer MAX30102 Pulse Sensor MQ-Series Gas Sensor I2C Telemetry & SOS
